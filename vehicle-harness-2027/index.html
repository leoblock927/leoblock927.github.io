2027 Vehicle Wire Harness

Wire Harness Project Manager, Longhorn Racing Solar. October 2026 to present In progress

12-pinstandard CAN pinout on every board
2dedicated shield pins, new for 2027
4conductors carrying power and CAN in one CarCAN cable

I'm leading the harness for the team's next car, built around its new 2027 boards. The last car's enclosure pinouts had no pin for CAN cable shielding, so shields were tied together with soldered jumpers. I wanted this harness to fix that at the pinout level and cut down on one-off pinouts.

Before routing anything, I modeled every connector on the car. Each board is grouped by its enclosure, its connectors come from the board's own design files, and pin names are normalized to one format across every board, so CAN, power, and ground mean the same thing everywhere.

From that model I set the architecture. Boards move to sealed Deutsch DTM connectors, with keyed panel mounts on each enclosure. CarCAN runs as a single 4-conductor cable carrying power and CAN, chaining enclosure to enclosure and ending at the battery box. Every board uses the same 12-pin CAN pinout with dedicated shield pins, and gets a panel-mount USB-C so it can be flashed without opening its enclosure.

LV power and supply pins get doubled wires to double the panel mount's current rating. The harness also covers the new lighting architecture, the charger path from wall AC through an off-car Elcon enclosure to the battery box, and both motor controller options.

I drew the schematic in harness.design and I'm now routing it in EPLAN Harness proD, starting with the battery box out to its panel mounts. I'm also working the BOM with an outside parts donor. Manufacturing starts in January, with the critical harness on the car in February.

On the firmware side, I wrote a thread-safe, mutex-protected RGB LED driver in embedded C for the team's STM32 FreeRTOS firmware so multiple tasks can share status LEDs, and stress-tested it with three tasks writing to one LED at once. The team now flashes it first on every new board to confirm the MCU has power and can be programmed.
