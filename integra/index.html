W5  Personal project

1994 Acura Integra

Restoration and modification. June 2023 to present

30 yrold factory harness, barely documented
4electrical jobs: CarPlay, grounds, camera, airbag
70+ hrinterior swap from tan to black

I picked this car with a design matrix, scoring six 1990s cars on reliability, community, style, and power. The Integra scored highest, and I spent a month finding one. It has a 30-year-old harness with very little documentation, so most electrical work on it starts with tracing circuits by hand before cutting anything.

I spliced and heat-shrank the adapter harness for a CarPlay head unit. It didn't power on at first, and after hours of checking connections I found the adapter's ground wasn't actually grounded. A new ground run straight to bare chassis metal fixed it, and I re-grounded other chassis circuits too. For the backup camera, I routed the video lines through the cabin and tapped power from the reverse light so it only runs in reverse.

I also installed a Japan-market Type-R steering wheel whose airbag connector didn't match the US car. I cut the US connector off the old airbag and spliced it onto the new wheel, and it plugged in with no airbag warning.

On the mechanical side, I replaced the radiator, fans, spark plugs, and water pump, ran a compression test, and installed adjustable coilovers and new front CV axles. I also swapped the whole interior from tan to black, sourcing parts from other owners, painting the pieces that never came in black, and building a suede headliner. I paid for parts by working during the week in high school and did the work on weekends.
