Data Acquisition Wire Harness

Wire Harness Project Manager, Longhorn Racing Solar. April to June 2026

14data acquisition PCBs connected
65 ftof cable routed through vehicle CAD
2%failure rate across 124 joints

The data acquisition harness connects 14 PCBs across 65 feet of cable. I routed it through the full vehicle CAD in EPlan, so every branch length came from the real car instead of a tape measure and a guess.

I produced 17 pages of schematics, pinouts, and manufacturing documentation, then led the build with teammates who were newer to harness work. We terminated everything by hand: crimping, soldering, and heat shrink on every joint.

I ran quality inspection on all 124 connector joints before installation, and the finished harness came in with a 2% defect rate. Because every wire was cut to its final CAD length, it went into the car and ran with zero on-track failures.
